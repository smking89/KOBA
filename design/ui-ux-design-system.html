Design Language

Color — forged & oxidized

Warm charcoal and parchment grounds, rust-orange as the single brand accent, brass as its metallic counterweight. Pulled from crate stencils and weathered survival-game HUDs rather than neon esports gradients.

Ink#1B1712
Paper#F4ECDD

Moss · success#5C6B4F
Steel · structure#48555F

Rarity ladder

Common → Relic. Used identically across products, auctions, cosmetics and shop distribution charts. Relic is the only tier that earns a glow — scarcity is felt, not just labeled.

Common Uncommon Rare Epic Legendary Relic

Typography

Slab display for headings (stamped crate-label character) · humanist sans for reading text · monospace for anything that must line up: KOBAIDs, prices, timers, ledgers.

Motion

Utilitarian, never decorative.

  • 120ms ease-out — button/press feedback
  • 200ms ease — tab & toggle transitions
  • 320ms ease-in-out — modal / sheet enter
  • Profile-effect rings pulse continuously — disabled under prefers-reduced-motion
  • No parallax, no auto-playing hero video

Iconography

Single-weight stroke, 1.6px, rounded joins, 24×24 grid. No filled glyphs except status dots.

Accessibility rules

  • Body text min 4.5:1 contrast against its surface in both themes; rarity chips checked independently since color-only signal is reinforced with text label + icon shape
  • Base font-size scales with OS text-size setting; layouts use rem, never fixed px for type
  • All rarity/status meaning is duplicated as text — never color alone
  • Focus rings visible on every interactive element (3px accent halo, never outline:none without replacement)
  • prefers-reduced-motion disables ring pulses, story auto-advance, and card hover-lift

Staff KOBAID format

Internal roles — no badge icon, ever. Identified only by format and by where they show up in this panel.

KOBA-SASuperadmin
KOBA-ADAdmin
KOBA-MDModerator

Group and shop-level "Admin" / "Moderator" badges are a different thing — community roles on a normal Player or Business KOBAID. Those keep their icon; see Group Page.

Issue a KOBAID

Superadmin & Admin only — registers a new role KOBAID directly to a user, bypassing self-serve onboarding.
